# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## What is expected

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. A review comment is about the change,
  not its author.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and move on.
- Prefer what is best for the project over what is best for you personally.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/dev-sriramp/polyvm/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel this repository has. Reports are reviewed and
handled as confidentially as the situation allows.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues and pull
requests that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain why when they
do. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
